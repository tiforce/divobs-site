## Banned Phrases

Avoid these words and phrases in launch copy:

- buy
- sell
- hold
- target price
- trade alert
- investment signal
- expected return
- guaranteed return
- stock pick
- portfolio advice
- personalized recommendation

Also avoid language that implies urgency, certainty, guaranteed outcomes, individualized suitability, or instructions for financial action.
