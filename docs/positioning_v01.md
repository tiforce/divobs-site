# Divergence Observatory Positioning v0.1

DRAFT - legal review required before production launch.

## What Divergence Observatory Is

Divergence Observatory is a non-advisory market structure observatory.

It is an anonymous research-lab style project focused on observing:

- divergence
- hidden stress
- cross-asset structure
- regime shifts
- volatility
- macro/geopolitical transmission

Its purpose is to describe observable structure, uncertainty, and context for informational and educational use.

## What Divergence Observatory Is Not

Divergence Observatory is not:

- an investment advisory service
- a stock-picking service
- a trading signal service
- a personalized financial advice product
- a brokerage, exchange, fund, or asset manager
- a system for telling users what financial decision to make

It does not provide investment advice, personalized financial advice, buy/sell/hold recommendations, price targets, trade alerts, guarantees of accuracy, or guarantees of future results.

## Non-Advisory Positioning

The brand should be framed around structured observation. The core idea is:

> Market structure, not market calls.

Divergence Observatory may describe observed conditions, uncertainty, and relationships across markets. It must not tell a reader what action to take with money, securities, derivatives, cryptoassets, commodities, or any other financial instrument.

## Safe Language

Preferred language:

- market structure observatory
- structured observation
- informational and educational use only
- observed divergence
- hidden stress
- cross-asset structure
- volatility conditions
- regime context
- macro/geopolitical transmission
- uncertainty remains
- legal review required before production launch

## Unsafe Language

Avoid language that implies:

- individualized suitability
- financial instructions
- specific transaction decisions
- certainty about outcomes
- guaranteed accuracy
- guaranteed future results
- urgency to act
- a forecast presented as a certainty
- private or privileged access to profitable decisions

Avoid these phrases:

- buy
- sell
- hold
- target price
- trade alert
- investment signal
- expected return
- guaranteed return
- stock pick
- portfolio advice
- personalized recommendation

## Observation vs Advice

Observation describes what is being seen and why it may matter structurally.

Advice tells a person what they should do.

Safe observation example:

> Volatility, credit, and currency channels are being monitored for signs of stress transmission.

Unsafe advice example:

> A reader should change a financial position because of this setup.

The first sentence describes a monitoring frame. The second sentence instructs financial action and must not be used.
