# Compliance Checklist v0.1

Use this checklist before future website, documentation, social, billing, or product changes.

## Content Safety

- [ ] No investment advice.
- [ ] No buy/sell/hold recommendations.
- [ ] No price targets.
- [ ] No trade alerts.
- [ ] No investment signals.
- [ ] No expected return language.
- [ ] No guaranteed performance language.
- [ ] No personalized recommendations.
- [ ] No paid plan promises investment outcomes.

## Product and Infrastructure Safety

- [ ] No live payments without approval.
- [ ] No external services without approval.
- [ ] No APIs, scraping, or market data ingestion without approval.
- [ ] No LLM calls or report prompts without approval.

## Launch Safety

- [ ] Legal review required before production launch.
