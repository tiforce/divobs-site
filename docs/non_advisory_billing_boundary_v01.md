# Non-Advisory Billing Boundary v0.1

DRAFT - legal review required before paid launch or production billing.

## Purpose

This document defines what Divergence Observatory may and may not sell in a future monetized access model.

It is a planning document only. It does not approve live payments, Stripe integration, checkout, subscription logic, APIs, authentication, databases, analytics, market data ingestion, LLM calls, or external services.

## Core Boundary

Divergence Observatory is a non-advisory market structure observatory.

Any future monetized access concept must remain informational and educational. It must not become investment advice, personalized financial advice, a trading signal service, a stock recommendation service, or portfolio advice.

## Allowed Future Concepts

Potential future access concepts may include:

- public educational market-structure notes
- research-format previews
- organizational access concepts
- non-personalized educational / structural context
- educational methodology notes
- non-personalized summaries of market-structure themes
- development updates about observatory formats

These concepts must not tell a reader what financial action to take.

## Not Allowed

Divergence Observatory must not sell or provide:

- investment advice
- personalized recommendations
- buy/sell/hold calls
- price targets
- portfolio construction
- trade alerts
- investment signals
- guaranteed outcomes
- individualized financial advice
- expected return claims
- profit or performance promises
- suitability assessments
- transaction instructions

## Wording Principles

Use language that describes observation, structure, uncertainty, and educational context.

Avoid language that implies:

- certainty
- urgency
- private trading advantage
- personalized suitability
- guaranteed accuracy
- guaranteed future results
- instructions to enter, exit, or modify a financial position
- better financial outcomes from paid access

## Billing-Page Wording Rules

Future billing pages, if approved, should:

- describe access as informational and educational
- state that content is non-advisory
- avoid suggesting that paid access improves investment results
- avoid language that sounds like alerts, signals, calls, picks, or recommendations
- avoid personalized suitability language
- make cancellation and refund terms clear
- avoid launching before legal and privacy review
- avoid implying that access creates a fiduciary, advisory, brokerage, or asset-management relationship

## CTA Rules

Safer CTA concepts:

- View access concepts
- Follow development status
- Review educational access options
- Continue to test-mode checkout
- Manage access

CTAs to avoid:

- Get signals
- Get alerts
- Trade now
- Beat the market
- Unlock picks
- Find the next winner
- Maximize returns
- Start profiting
- Build your portfolio

## Safer Wording Examples

Safer examples:

- Access to educational market-structure notes.
- Non-advisory structural context for informational use.
- Additional research-format previews, subject to legal review.
- Organizational access concept for internal educational review.
- Content describes observed structure and uncertainty; it does not provide recommendations.
- No investment advice, personalized financial advice, price targets, trade alerts, or investment signals are provided.

## Prohibited Wording Examples

Do not use:

- Buy this setup.
- Sell before the move.
- Hold this asset.
- Our target price is...
- This is a trade alert.
- This is an investment signal.
- This stock is a pick.
- This portfolio allocation is suitable for you.
- Paid members get profitable calls.
- Subscribe to improve your returns.
- Guaranteed market insight.
- Act now before the market moves.

## Access Concept Examples

Allowed framing:

- Public: public observatory notes and development updates.
- Research Format Preview: additional educational market-structure notes, subject to legal review.
- Organizational Concept: future organizational review concept, subject to legal approval.

Avoid framing:

- Pro signals
- Premium trades
- Institutional calls
- Alerts package
- Portfolio recommendations
- High-conviction picks

## Review Requirements

Before any future monetized access launch:

- legal review is required
- privacy review is required
- billing copy review is required
- refund and cancellation policy review is required
- Tokushoho / Specified Commercial Transactions Act review may be required
- owner approval for production billing is required
