# Codex Project Rules

This project is compliance-first.

Divergence Observatory must remain positioned as an informational and educational market structure observatory.

## Do Not Add

- Do not add investment advice.
- Do not add buy/sell/hold recommendations.
- Do not add price targets.
- Do not add trade alerts.
- Do not add investment signals.
- Do not add expected return or guaranteed return language.
- Do not add personalized financial advice.
- Do not add live payments, Stripe checkout, subscription logic, or billing code without explicit approval.
- Do not add APIs, scraping, market data ingestion, LLM calls, auth, database, analytics, tracking scripts, or external services without explicit approval.

## Required Positioning

- Keep Divergence Observatory focused on market structure, divergence, hidden stress, cross-asset structure, regime shifts, volatility, and macro/geopolitical transmission.
- Keep all content informational and educational.
- Avoid language that could be interpreted as financial instructions, individualized suitability, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Launch Rule

Legal review is required before production launch.
